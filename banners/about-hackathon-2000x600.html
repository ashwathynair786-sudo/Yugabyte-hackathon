One agent’s learning becomes every agent’s advantage.

Every agentic app is built from models, tools, governance and context. Models are a market and tools are being standardized by MCP. Context is still hand-rolled from scratch, and it decides whether everything else pays off.

Meko gives your agents one universal, governed context. It connects through a single MCP endpoint to the tools you already use, scores every retrieval for relevance, explains what is missing, and learns over time.

Start free, build from anywhere, and show us what agents can do when they stop forgetting.
