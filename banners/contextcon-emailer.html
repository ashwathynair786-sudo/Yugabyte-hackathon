Most AI agents forget everything the moment a session ends. ContextCon is your chance to build one that doesn’t, and compete for a $10,000 prize pool while you’re at it.

So, what is ContextCon?

A worldwide virtual hackathon from Meko by YugabyteDB. Build agents with shared memory, knowledge, and full decision traces on Meko, the self-learning context engine. Connect any MCP client (Claude, Cursor, Codex, LangChain, CrewAI, Base44, and more) to one endpoint, with no stitched-together stack of vector DB, memory store, and RAG glue.

Why Meko? Because agents get better when one learns, every agent can use them. Meko consolidates the 5+ systems agentic apps usually need into one governed datapack
