logo-system

Logo system

Meko is a YugabyteDB product, not an independent brand. The endorsement is the default, and the monogram is the only sanctioned exception.

Variants

All four lockups carry the endorsement. Only the monogram omits it.

Monogram — the endorsement exception

Shown with the platform corner mask applied. Ship the artwork square and full-bleed — never bake in the radius.

Export corrected 31 July 2026. The previous SVG set was missing both lowercase y glyphs in “yugabyteDB” — 14 paths where 16 are required. The Illustrator master was intact; the fault was in the SVG export. The artwork shown here is regenerated from that master. Replace any deployed copies, including the site header and favicon.

Rename the source files. The current names Meko_Logo_Color-Lght-Bkg and Color-Drk-Bkg imply the orange belongs to Meko. It doesn’t. Use Endorsed and Mono instead.

Minimum sizes

Derived from the artwork: the “By yugabyteDB” line occupies 13.7% of the lockup’s height and 5.9% of its width. Below these sizes it stops being legible, and the monogram takes over.

MediumEndorsed lockup minimumBelow that
Screen180 px wideMonogram
Print45 mm wideMonogram

These are conservative — the measurement includes descenders, so x-height is smaller still. Round up, never down.

The wordmark is artwork, never type

The wordmark is custom lettering. It is not set in Bloka or any other typeface and cannot be reproduced by typing. Never retype “meko” and treat it as the logo. Never extend the monogram to other letters by setting them. Always place the supplied files.

This error is unusually easy to make, because Bloka is a geometric face sitting next to a geometric wordmark. Retyped, it looks almost right.

Clear space and placement

Reserve clear space equal to the height of the wordmark’s m on all sides. Nothing enters that zone — no text, no rules, no texture detail, no partner marks.

On textured grounds the logo sits in the image’s clear zone, never over the busy area. See imagery direction.

Monogram construction

The monogram is the wordmark’s own m, optically centred with symmetric side padding at 16.5% of the canvas, clearing the platform corner radius. The existing geometry is correct and should not be redrawn or re-centred.

The rule: orange appears only where it already appears — on the YugabyteDB mark inside the endorsement. If a design needs a warm colour, that’s a request to extend the Meko palette, not permission to borrow the parent’s.

Purple’s two headline ratios clear the 4.5 threshold by almost nothing. Fine at body size and above — don’t tint it, don’t shrink it, and re-test after any CMYK conversion. Purple and green never sit on each other; put a neutral between them.

Gradients

The gradient is defined by its axis, not its hues: 135°, light at top-left running to dark at bottom-right. Both app icons use this construction with different colours, which is what makes it a system rather than a single asset.

GradientStopsAngle
Purple#7859fe → #4a35a2 → #20154f135°
Black#000000 → #17161b → #4b4754135°

Boundary: gradients are for expressive surfaces only — icons, full-bleed grounds, key art. Everything functional uses flat values: type, UI, cards, buttons, borders. Without this rule every surface becomes a gradient and none of them mean anything.

Three faces, three jobs. Bloka is the only licensed face and appears in titles only.

Specimens below are outlined vector paths, not live font rendering. They reproduce Bloka exactly at any size with no font software in this file — the same reasoning that makes the wordmark safe to distribute. fsType 0 means a PDF export may legitimately embed a subset, but @font-face web use is governed by the Digitype EULA rather than the embedding bits, so it stays out of the HTML.

Bloka — titles

Uppercase, tight tracking — the site H1 runs at -1.8px at 80px. The full family is licensed, so the synthesised 600 on the site is a build oversight: Bloka-SemiBold.ttf exists and simply isn’t loaded.

Inter — body and UI

Meko is a data-persistence platform for hybrid teams of agents and humans, delivered over a remote MCP endpoint. It keeps shared context in one place, so you don’t stitch together your own vector store, graph store, RAG pipeline, relational database and object store.

Roboto Mono — code and machine voice

› where were we?

Code samples, MCP endpoints, terminal output, agent responses. Apache 2.0 — load a bold weight if the scale needs one.

Brand subset

The family offers 18 styles. Using all of them would dissolve the identity, so the brand uses three:

WeightUse
400 regularLarge display lines where Bold reads too heavy.
600 semiboldSection headings. Load the file rather than letting the browser fake it.
700 boldPrimary headline weight, uppercase, tracked tight.

Thin through Light are unusable at headline scale on dark grounds — the strokes disappear. ExtraBold and Black are close enough to Bold to add nothing. Italics are not part of the system; a geometric display italic is rarely used well and there is no job for it here.

The machine voice

Roboto Mono and green are one device, not two rules. Mono type plus #51d977 on a dark ground marks something as machine-spoken: a command, a returned value, an agent’s output. Used together they signal. Used separately and decoratively, neither signals anything.

Bloka and Inter carry the human voice. Mono and green carry the system’s. Keep that division intact.

Retired

Roobert and Albert Sans are removed from the system. Roobert should be stripped from the theme — it currently sets roughly 35 elements on the homepage, and dropping it removes a paid, non-transferable license from the stack.

imagery

Imagery direction

Specified as a treatment, not as files. Any image meeting the rules below is compliant, so assets can be swapped without redoing layouts.

clear zone — min 40%

The clear zone is not empty space. It is what the negative space is for — it holds the logo at full contrast.

AttributeRule
Ground#000000. The image sits on and dissolves into pure black.
PaletteCool only — blues, violets, lilacs, white. Fine warm speckle acceptable as incidental detail.
FormFluid, marbled, organic flow. Not geometric, not gridded, not 3D-rendered, not photographic.
CompositionAt least 40% of the frame stays clear near-black on one side — left by default. Texture bleeds off the opposite edge. Never centred, never uniformly busy.
Clear zoneMust hold the wordmark or monogram at full contrast.
ProhibitedWarm-dominant palettes, recognisable objects, faces, text, and any use inside the logo or lockup.

Current assets are licensed stock and interim. Envato Elements, certificate XBWSU8MTKJ, non-exclusive and revocable. They will be replaced by a commissioned proprietary asset. Keep them out of anything durable — no long-shelf-life print, no merchandise, nothing embedded in a trademark filing. The table above doubles as the commissioning brief.

Partner logos

Every partner mark is governed by its owner’s brand guidelines, not by this document. Meko’s rules sit on top of theirs, never instead of them.

Rules

Source filesAlways the partner’s official asset from their own brand page. Never traced, recoloured, or rebuilt from a screenshot.
ColourPartner marks keep their own colour. Do not force them into the Meko palette. Where a monochrome set is officially provided, that set may be used for visual consistency in a row of marks.
SizingOptically balanced, not mathematically equal. Match apparent weight rather than bounding box.
Clear spaceWhichever is larger: the partner’s stated clear space, or Meko’s.
RelationshipPartner marks indicate interoperability. They must never be arranged to imply endorsement, partnership, or joint ownership unless a written agreement says so.
NeverLock a partner mark to the Meko logo. Place a partner mark inside the endorsement. Animate or distort a partner mark.

Two open items on this section. First, “coming soon” claims need a named internal owner who approves them — they carry marketing and legal exposure. Second, confirm per-partner whether trademark use requires notice or permission; several of these companies require attribution language or restrict use in marketing entirely. This section is provisional until that audit exists.

Motion

This section is a stub. Motion exists in the brand but has never been specified, so what follows is a starting position rather than a standard.

What exists today

Homepage heromeko-data-hero.mp4
Menu iconsGradient SVGs for Discord and GitHub
ImpliedThe fluid textures read as frozen frames of motion

Proposed principles

Meko’s subject is context accumulating and being shared. Motion should express continuity and flow — things persisting, moving between places, and settling — rather than snapping, bouncing, or announcing itself.

CharacterFluid and continuous. Eased, never linear. No bounce, no overshoot, no elastic.
DirectionFollow the 135° gradient axis where a direction is needed. Reinforce the one axis the brand already has.
DurationInterface feedback under 200 ms. Transitions 200–400 ms. Ambient loops slow enough not to draw the eye.
Machine voiceTyped and terminal-like — the cursor, the prompt, text appearing as if returned. This is the one place a mechanical rhythm is correct.
AccessibilityEverything ambient or decorative respects prefers-reduced-motion. Non-negotiable.

Needed before this section is real: a decision on whether motion is a brand system or per-project treatment; easing curves as actual values; whether the hero video is a reusable asset or one-off; and rules for the product UI, which currently has no motion spec at all.

applications

Applications

How the system resolves on the surfaces that exist today.

Website hero

Pure black ground. Texture bleeding from the right, logo in the clear left zone. Bloka uppercase title, tight tracking. Purple for the primary action.

Blog banner

#121017 card on textured ground. Wordmark with endorsement top-left. Headline in mono green with cursor — the machine voice, used correctly. Rules above and below in white.

App icon and favicon

Monogram only, no endorsement. Purple or black gradient at 135°. Full bleed, no baked-in corner radius — platforms mask it.

Social avatar

Monogram. Test at 32 px before shipping; anything with the endorsement fails at avatar scale.

Docs and product UI

Inter throughout, mono for code and endpoints, purple for links and active states. Flat values only — no gradients in functional chrome.

Stickers and print

Endorsed lockup at 45 mm minimum. Supply artwork at 300 dpi with bleed. Re-test purple after CMYK conversion — saturated violets shift.

do-and-dont

Do’s and don’ts

Every item below is a mistake that has already been made, or is one step away from being made.

Do

Place the supplied logo files.

Don’t

Retype “meko” in Bloka. The wordmark is custom lettering and will look almost, but not quite, right.

Do

Use green on black or #121017.

Don’t

Put green on white (1.82:1) or on purple (3.56:1). No size or weight fixes it.

Do

Treat orange as YugabyteDB’s, appearing only inside the endorsement.

Don’t

Use orange as a Meko accent, button, link or highlight because a layout wants warmth.

Do

Reserve gradients for icons, grounds and key art.

Don’t

Gradient the buttons, cards, borders or body text.

Do

Drop to the monogram below 180 px or 45 mm.

Don’t

Shrink the endorsed lockup until “By yugabyteDB” turns to mush.

Do

Sit the logo in the image’s clear zone.

Don’t

Centre the logo over the busy part of a texture.

Do

Write in sentence case, everywhere.

Don’t

Title Case Every Word Including Articles. And never “Chatgpt”.

Do

State limits plainly, as the FAQ does.

Don’t

Polish out the candour. “Seamless”, “unlock”, “empower” — none of them.

Do

Attach a workload and a source to any performance number.

Don’t

Publish an unvalidated multiple. The 15× token claim is withdrawn and must not be reused.

Do

Use partners’ official assets in their own colours.

Don’t

Recolour a partner mark to fit the palette, or lock it to the Meko logo.
